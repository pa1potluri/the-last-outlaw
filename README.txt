THE LAST OUTLAW - ENHANCED FIXED EDITION

START
Double-click "Play The Last Outlaw.bat" in this folder. Python 3 required.
Keep its console window open while playing. Ctrl+C there stops the server.
If the game is already open, press F5 to load the update.

TITLE SCREEN
Space / Enter / click: start a new campaign (replaces the saved campaign)
C: continue your saved campaign from the latest checkpoint
H: switch Standard / Hard before starting a new campaign

PLAY
A/D or Left/Right: move. Shift: sprint / spur your horse.
W/S or Up/Down: change lane. Space: jump / skip chapter card.
J or hold mouse: fire. Q or 1-5: switch weapon. E: interact.
M: mute/unmute. P or Escape / on-screen button: pause/resume.

MISSION GOALS
Complete the checklist to open the exit. Approach captives in their lane to
rescue them. Gold diamonds mark supplies, documents and sabotage targets;
stand nearby in their lane and press E after defeating the leader.
You can travel left again to finish missed tasks.

WHAT IS NEW
- Mixed sprite orientations corrected: enemies face the player in each pose.
- Separate health, ammo, bounty, chapter title and mission panels.
- Warm mine lighting, atmosphere particles and restrained edge shading.
- Guards warn before changing lanes. Boss attacks mark dangerous lanes.
- Saloon boss fires double shots; mountain boss uses shotgun volleys.
- Timed horseback mission. Hard mode gives less time and stronger attacks.
- Illustrated armored war wagon: dodge, then shoot its exposed vent.
- Iron Marshal changes patterns as his health falls. Jump the all-lane volley.
- Quick kill streaks earn extra bounty; getting hurt breaks the streak.
- Checkpoint autosave and Continue, including mission and inventory progress.
- Bounded visual effects and cleared blurred-background caches between levels.
- Earlier mission, collision, ammo, timer, pause, audio and launcher fixes retained.

SAVES
Saves use this browser's local storage at the game's address. Continue resumes
at the checkpoint with full health, preserving saved kills, objectives, ammo
and bounty; it does not restore a projectile-by-projectile combat frame.
Before your first checkpoint, Continue resumes at the start of the saved level.
Progress saves on checkpoints/interactions and periodically after a checkpoint.
Use the same browser and address. Clearing browser data removes the save.
Standard and Hard share one campaign save. Developer/test mode does not save.
The launcher prefers 127.0.0.1:8831; if busy, a different port is used and that
address has its own browser storage.

PHONE (LANDSCAPE)
The game fills the screen. Drag the thumb stick to move; push far to sprint
and flick up/down to change lane. Tap Jump or hold Shoot. Tap the WEAPON
panel to switch guns and MISSION to use a nearby objective. Pause contains
Sound On/Off and Resume. Rotate to landscape to play. No gamepad controls.
The original game folder and original art/sounds have not been modified.
A new illustrated wagon asset was added to this fixed copy.
See FIXES-AND-TESTS.md for the scope and validation details.
